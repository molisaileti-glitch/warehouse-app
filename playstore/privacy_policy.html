SB Warehouse Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

SB Warehouse is an inventory and warehouse operations application for crop receiving, stock tracking, worker management, receipt printing, and synchronization with the ShambaBora backend.

Information We Collect

The app may collect account details such as name, email address, phone number, role, user identifiers, and login credentials. It may also collect business, warehouse, worker, farmer, dependant, organization, crop, harvest, stock, dispatch, stock count, stock adjustment, bag tag, bag weight, moisture, receipt, and timestamp information entered or generated while using the app.

The app may request Bluetooth, USB, network, and location-related permissions so users can connect weighing scales, printers, moisture meters, detect network availability, and record warehouse location details.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to provide warehouse and inventory functionality, manage user accounts, support offline records, synchronize data with the backend, maintain traceability, print receipts, and help users verify crop stock and warehouse activities.

Sharing

Data is sent to the ShambaBora backend for app functionality and account management. We do not sell user data or use it for advertising.

Security

The app uses HTTPS for backend communication and stores operational records locally to support offline use. Users should keep their login credentials private and use trusted devices.

Account And Data Deletion

Users can request account and associated data deletion using this form: https://forms.gle/w4AaDT2U3qriBeP76.

Children

SB Warehouse is intended for business and warehouse operations users. It is not directed to children.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, use the account deletion form above or contact the organization that provided your SB Warehouse account.
